When you first start working out, it's essential to fuel your body with the right foods to support your fitness goals. Eating the right foods will help you recover from your workouts, build muscle, and increase your energy levels. In this article, we'll discuss what to eat when you first start working out, including the best foods for pre-workout, post-workout, and meal planning.

## Introduction to Fitness Nutrition
Fitness nutrition is a crucial aspect of any workout routine. When you're new to working out, it can be overwhelming to figure out what to eat and when. However, with a little knowledge and planning, you can optimize your diet to support your fitness goals. One of the most important things to consider is your macronutrient intake. Macronutrients are the three main categories of nutrients: carbohydrates, protein, and fat. Aim to eat a balanced diet that includes a mix of all three.

## Pre-Workout Nutrition
Pre-workout nutrition is critical to fuel your workouts and prevent fatigue. Aim to eat a meal or snack that includes a mix of carbohydrates and protein about 1-2 hours before your workout. Some good pre-workout food options include:
| Food | Carbohydrates | Protein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banana | 30g | 2g |
| Apple | 25g | 0.5g |
| Greek Yogurt | 10g | 20g |
| Whole Wheat Toast | 30g | 5g |

## Post-Workout Nutrition
Post-workout nutrition is also crucial to help your body recover from your workout. Aim to eat a meal or snack that includes a mix of carbohydrates and protein within 30-60 minutes after your workout. Some good post-workout food options include:
| Food | Carbohydrates | Protein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Protein Shake | 10g | 30g |
| Chicken and Rice | 40g | 30g |
| Whole Wheat Pasta | 60g | 20g |
<ProTip>Make sure to also include healthy fats in your post-workout meal, such as nuts or seeds, to help with recovery and muscle growth.</ProTip>

## Meal Planning for Fitness
Meal planning is an essential part of any fitness routine. Aim to eat 5-6 meals per day, spaced out every 2-3 hours. This will help keep your energy levels stable and support muscle growth. ## Staying Hydrated
Staying hydrated is critical to support your fitness goals. Aim to drink at least 8-10 glasses of water per day, and more if you're working out regularly. You can also consider adding electrolyte supplements to your water to help replenish lost electrolytes. Check out our article on [best electrolyte supplements for endurance athletes](/blog/best-electrolyte-supplements-for-endurance-athletes) for more information.

## Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What should I eat before a workout?
A: Aim to eat a meal or snack that includes a mix of carbohydrates and protein about 1-2 hours before your workout.
Q: How much protein should I eat per day?
A: The exact amount of protein you should eat per day will depend on your fitness goals and individual needs. Generally, aim to eat 1-1.5 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day.
Q: What are the best supplements for fitness?
A: Some popular supplements for fitness include protein powder, creatine, and pre-workout. When choosing a supplement, make sure to read the label carefully and look for third-party testing and certification.
Q: How often should I eat per day?
A: Aim to eat 5-6 meals per day, spaced out every 2-3 hours. This will help keep your energy levels stable and support muscle growth.
Q: What are the best foods for post-workout recovery?
A: Some good post-workout food options include protein shakes, chicken and rice, and whole wheat pasta. Make sure to also include healthy fats in your post-workout meal to help with recovery and muscle growth.

By following these tips and guidelines, you can optimize your diet to support your fitness goals. Remember to stay hydrated, eat a balanced diet, and consider adding supplements to your routine. With patience and consistency, you can achieve your fitness goals and live a healthier, happier life.
