## How to Use Whey Protein for Muscle Building
To get the most out of whey protein for muscle building, it's essential to use it correctly. Here are some tips:
* Take 1-2 scoops of whey protein within 30-60 minutes after exercise to help promote muscle growth and repair.
* Mix whey protein with water or a low-calorie beverage to keep calorie intake in check.
* Choose a whey protein powder that is unflavored or naturally flavored to avoid artificial sweeteners and flavors.

While whey protein is generally considered safe, there are some potential side effects to be aware of. These include:
