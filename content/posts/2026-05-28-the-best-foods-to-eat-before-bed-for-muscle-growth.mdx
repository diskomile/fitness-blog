## Introduction to Muscle Growth and Nutrition
Muscle growth, also known as muscle hypertrophy, is the process of increasing muscle mass through resistance training and proper nutrition. Adequate nutrition is essential for muscle growth, as it provides the necessary building blocks for muscle repair and growth. A well-balanced diet that includes protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats can help support muscle growth. ## The Importance of Pre-Bedtime Nutrition
Pre-bedtime nutrition is critical for muscle growth and recovery. During sleep, the body repairs and rebuilds muscle tissue, and adequate nutrition is necessary to support this process. Eating the right foods before bed can help promote muscle growth, reduce muscle soreness, and improve overall recovery. A good pre-bedtime snack should include a combination of protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats. <ProTip> Aim to consume a snack that includes 20-30 grams of protein, 30-40 grams of carbohydrates, and 10-20 grams of healthy fats within 30-60 minutes of bedtime.</ProTip>

## Best Foods to Eat Before Bed for Muscle Growth
Some of the best foods to eat before bed for muscle growth include:
* Greek yogurt with berries and nuts
* Cottage cheese with fruit
* Protein shakes with casein protein
* Turkey or chicken with sweet potatoes and avocado
* Whole grain crackers with almond butter and banana slices
These foods provide a combination of protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats that can help promote muscle growth and recovery.
