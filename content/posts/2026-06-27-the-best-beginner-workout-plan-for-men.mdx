## Introduction to Weightlifting
Weightlifting is an essential component of any workout plan, and it's crucial to start with the basics. As a beginner, it's recommended to focus on compound exercises such as squats, deadlifts, and bench press. These exercises work multiple muscle groups at once, helping you build overall strength and muscle mass. For more information on creating a calorie deficit and losing weight, check out our article on [how to create a calorie deficit without starving](/blog/how-to-create-a-calorie-deficit-without-starving).

## Setting Up Your Workout Routine
A typical beginner workout plan for men consists of 3-4 days of weightlifting per week, with at least one day of rest in between. It's essential to start with lighter weights and progressively increase the load as you become stronger. A good rule of thumb is to follow the principle of [progressive overload](/blog/progressive-overload-the-key-to-continuous-gains), which involves increasing the weight or reps over time to challenge your muscles.

## Sample Workout Routine
Here's a sample workout routine for beginners:
- Day 1: Chest and Triceps
  - Barbell Bench Press (3 sets of 8-12 reps)
  - Incline Dumbbell Press (3 sets of 10-15 reps)
  - Tricep Pushdown (3 sets of 12-15 reps)
- Day 2: Back and Biceps
  - Pull-ups (3 sets of 8-12 reps)
  - Barbell Rows (3 sets of 8-12 reps)
  - Dumbbell Bicep Curls (3 sets of 10-15 reps)
- Day 3: Legs
  - Squats (3 sets of 8-12 reps)
  - Leg Press (3 sets of 10-15 reps)
  - Lunges (3 sets of 10-15 reps per leg)
- Day 4: Shoulders and Abs
  - Dumbbell Shoulder Press (3 sets of 8-12 reps)
  - Lateral Raises (3 sets of 10-15 reps)
  - Plank (3 sets of 30-60 seconds)

## Warm-Up and Recovery
A proper warm-up and recovery routine is essential for preventing injuries and supporting muscle growth. Before each workout, make sure to warm up with 5-10 minutes of cardio and dynamic stretching. After your workout, take the time to stretch and foam roll your muscles to aid in recovery. For more information on the best warm-up routine, check out our article on [best warm-up routine before lifting](/blog/best-warm-up-routine-before-lifting).

## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are some common questions and answers for beginners:
- Q: How often should I work out?
  A: As a beginner, it's recommended to work out 3-4 times per week, with at least one day of rest in between.
- Q: What's the best way to lose weight?
  A: The best way to lose weight is to create a calorie deficit through a combination of diet and exercise. Check out our article on [how to create a calorie deficit without starving](/blog/how-to-create-a-calorie-deficit-without-starving) for more information.
- Q: How much protein should I consume?
  A: As a beginner, it's recommended to consume at least 1g of protein per pound of body weight daily.
- Q: What's the best supplement for muscle growth?
  A: The best supplement for muscle growth is a combination of protein powder and creatine. 
- Q: How long does it take to see results?
  A: Results can vary depending on individual factors, but with a consistent workout routine and proper nutrition, you can expect to see noticeable gains within 6-12 weeks.
- Q: What's the best way to track progress?
  A: The best way to track progress is to take progress photos, measurements, and track your workouts and nutrition.
