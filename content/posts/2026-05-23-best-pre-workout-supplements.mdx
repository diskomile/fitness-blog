## Pre-Workout Supplement Safety and Side Effects
While pre-workout supplements can be safe and effective when used properly, they can also have potential side effects. Common side effects include jitters, anxiety, and stomach upset, which can be caused by high levels of caffeine or other ingredients. It's essential to follow the recommended dosage and start with a small amount to assess your tolerance.
