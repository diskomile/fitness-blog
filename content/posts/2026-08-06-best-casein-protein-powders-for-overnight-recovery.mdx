## What is Casein Protein?
Casein protein is a type of protein found in milk, making up about 80% of its protein content. It's a slow-digesting protein, meaning it takes longer to break down and absorb than other types of protein like whey. This makes it ideal for **overnight recovery**, as it provides a sustained release of amino acids to support muscle growth and repair.
