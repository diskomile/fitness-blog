## What are Collagen Peptides?
Collagen peptides are short chains of amino acids that are derived from collagen, a protein found in connective tissue. They are typically obtained through hydrolysis, a process that breaks down collagen into smaller peptides. These peptides can be easily absorbed by the body, making them an effective supplement for supporting joint health and recovery.

## Benefits of Collagen Peptides for Joints
Collagen peptides have been shown to have numerous benefits for joint health, including reducing inflammation, improving joint lubrication, and promoting the growth of new cartilage. They can also help to reduce joint pain and stiffness, making them an effective supplement for individuals with osteoarthritis or other joint-related disorders.
