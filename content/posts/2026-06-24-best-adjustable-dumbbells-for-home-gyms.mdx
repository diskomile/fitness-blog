## Introduction to Adjustable Dumbbells
Adjustable dumbbells are designed to replace multiple sets of traditional dumbbells, making them a convenient and space-saving solution for home gyms. They typically consist of a single unit with adjustable weight settings, allowing you to quickly switch between different weights. This feature makes them ideal for those who want to perform a variety of exercises without having to purchase multiple sets of dumbbells.

## Benefits of Adjustable Dumbbells
The benefits of adjustable dumbbells are numerous. For one, they save space, which is a major concern for those with limited room in their home gym. They also provide convenience, as you can quickly switch between different weights without having to change dumbbells. Additionally, adjustable dumbbells can be more cost-effective than purchasing multiple sets of traditional dumbbells. To get the most out of your adjustable dumbbells, consider creating a [push-pull-legs routine](/blog/how-to-build-a-push-pull-legs-routine) that targets all major muscle groups.
